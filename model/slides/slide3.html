Every filmmaker gets recommendations matched to how they hire, which could halve the time they spend searching for crew

Filmmaker typeHow they hire todayWhat the app recommends
1 · Trusted networkCalls people they already knowRe-book: people they have worked with, and people those crew members recommend
2 · Open searchPosts publicly and asks for referralsA feed of new people who fit the role
3 · ScrambleTries everything; hardest roles to fillA hand-picked shortlist of available, reviewed people

When a filmmaker posts a job, the app looks at how they found crew before and picks the matching type of recommendations.
